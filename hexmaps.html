Battle maps

◂ Back to the game

Every city has a 13 × 12 hex battlefield of about 5 km hexes cut from the real ground around it: hillshaded relief at 130 m detail from the same elevation data as the strategic map, the true courses of rivers and coast, the walls at the centre, and roads leaving toward the cities that neighbour it on the strategic map. Attackers deploy at the road exit on the side their city lies; defenders hold the walls. Generated by tools/buildhex.js.

Deployment

Proposed rules: movement points per hex, the defence bonus to a unit standing in it, whether it blocks line of sight for archers, and whether cavalry may charge through it. Rivers are crossed only at bridges and fords (the pale dotted road) or by fleet-borne troops; mountains are impassable except along roads.
